# Thinking Bridge 3.2.1
### Think in the language. Don't translate it.

Most language apps teach you to swap words. Thinking Bridge teaches you to
*build sentences the way the language builds them*, so that Arabic's verb-first
rhythm, Japanese's final verbs, and German's second-position rule become
instincts instead of puzzles. This is our first official release, and it
arrives on every screen you own.

## What's inside

**One idea, sixteen languages.** English, Modern Standard Arabic, Egyptian,
Levantine, Gulf and Moroccan Arabic, German, Japanese, Spanish, French,
Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Mandarin, Hindi and Greek. Any language can be
your starting point; any language can be your destination.

**The Matching Engine.** 240 directional language pairs are scored against
your goals, your pace and your appetite for challenge, weighing vocabulary
overlap, script, word order and morphology, and every recommendation shows
its reasoning.

**Real vocabulary.** Thousands of genuine dictionary entries per language,
drawn from Wiktionary via Kaikki.org, graded from A0 to C2.

**A habit that sticks.** Spaced-repetition flashcards, daily quests, weekly
leagues, achievements and streak protection.

**Yours to share.** One tap to share your progress to WhatsApp, and a reviews
tab that reaches us directly.

**Offline-first.** Learn on a train, in a village, on a plane.

## Honest notes
- Egyptian, Levantine, Gulf and Moroccan Arabic are in **beta**. Their
  dictionaries currently share a large core of Modern Standard Arabic while
  dialect-specific vocabulary grows.
- Desktop installers are not yet code-signed. Your system may show a warning:
  choose "More info → Run anyway" (Windows) or right-click → Open (macOS).
