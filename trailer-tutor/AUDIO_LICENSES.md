# Audio provenance

No third-party audio recordings or music tracks are used. The five sound types (question tick, four rising counter dings, low thud, C6 note with quiet echoes, and two-note chime) are synthesized by `audio/build_sfx.py` specifically for this animatic. There are no external license URLs or attribution requirements.

The generated audio file is mono 48 kHz PCM. The AAC audio in the final MP4 peaks at approximately −6 dBFS. Frames 660–689 (22–23 seconds) are digitally silent. There is no music bed, no reveal sound, and no end-card sting.
